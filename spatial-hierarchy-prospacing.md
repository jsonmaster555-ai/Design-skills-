# Spatial Hierarchy & Prospacing

Spatial hierarchy uses distance, position, alignment, padding, margins, and negative space to communicate relationships and importance. Prospacing means treating spacing as a deliberate system rather than random empty space.

Small distances communicate strong relationships. Larger distances communicate separation. Internal component spacing should generally be tighter than spacing between separate groups.

## Spacing levels

Distinguish micro spacing, component spacing, group spacing, section spacing, and page spacing. Do not solve page-level breathing room by inflating buttons, inputs, cards, tabs, or navigation rows.

A useful starting scale is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, and 128px. Not every value must be used. Equivalent relationships should generally receive equivalent spacing.

## Proximity before containers

Before adding a border, card, background, or divider, determine whether proximity can communicate the grouping more quietly.

## Optical spacing

Mathematical equality is not always optical equality. Irregular icons, letterforms, and circular shapes may need small corrections. Start from the spacing system and then make restrained optical adjustments.

## Density

Productivity software, editors, dashboards, and developer tools often benefit from tighter density than marketing pages. Compact does not mean cramped, and spacious does not mean oversized.

Spacing is structure. Ask what relationship each gap communicates.
